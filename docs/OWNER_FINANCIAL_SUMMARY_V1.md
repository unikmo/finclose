# Owner Financial Summary V1

## Product rule

Every completed FinClose book close must have two outputs:

1. Finished books / accountant close package — detailed accounting records and evidence for the accountant.
2. Owner Financial Summary — the same final figures translated into plain language for the business owner.

The owner report is part of the product, not an optional analytics add-on.

## What the owner sees

### How the business performed
- Sales
- Direct costs, when applicable
- Operating costs
- Other income/costs, when applicable
- Income-tax expense, when applicable
- Profit or loss

### Where the business stands
- Cash
- Money customers still owe
- Other assets
- Supplier bills still to pay
- Taxes still to pay
- Payroll amounts still to pay
- Loans and other liabilities
- Owner equity

### What changed
When a prior comparable period is available, FinClose explains the change in sales, profit, cash, receivables and payables in both amount and percentage terms.

### What needs attention
Only factual, accounting-derived items are highlighted: a recorded loss, negative cash, unpaid customer balances, supplier payables, tax liabilities, payroll liabilities and loans. V1 does not invent a business-health score.

## Integrity rules

- Never call bank inflows "sales" or bank outflows "expenses" without the final accounting classification.
- Never derive profit from cash movement.
- The statement can be FINAL only from accountant-signed-off or approved-and-locked books.
- Assets must equal liabilities + equity exactly before a final owner statement is produced.
- The report must identify the source close/evidence it was generated from.
- The owner report does not replace the detailed books, statutory accounts, tax filings or accountant sign-off.

## UX principle

Use business-owner language first. Accounting terminology may appear only where it improves precision, with a plain-English explanation beside it.

Preferred examples:
- "Customers owe you" instead of only "Accounts receivable".
- "You owe suppliers" instead of only "Accounts payable".
- "Taxes still to pay" instead of only "Tax liabilities".
- "Owner value in the business" alongside equity.
