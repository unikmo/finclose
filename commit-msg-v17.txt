payroll(us): add Wisconsin -- full 50-state-plus-DC coverage (v17)

Wisconsin was the last remaining gap. A prior pass this session (v16)
deliberately declined to ship it after finding a top-bracket formula in
the WI Form 1 ANNUAL RETURN instructions, correctly judging that
mixing annual-return constants into a withholding calculation without
confirming interchangeability was too risky.

This pass found the actual answer: Wisconsin's own current Publication
W-166 (1/26), "Withholding Tax Guide" -- the department's dedicated
payroll-withholding publication, distinct from the annual-return
instructions -- contains a section titled "ALTERNATE METHOD OF
WITHHOLDING WISCONSIN INCOME TAX" with a complete percentage-method
formula: a deduction phase-out by filing status (matching the figures
already confirmed in an earlier pass), a flat $400-per-exemption
subtraction, and a single 4-bracket schedule (3.54% / 4.65% / 5.30% /
7.65%) that the guide's own worked examples confirm applies to both
single and married filers alike.

All 3 of the guide's own worked examples are reproduced exactly by
this implementation; the self-test suite hand-verifies Example 3
(married, biweekly $1,000, 3 exemptions -> $22.08) end-to-end through
the actual calculateUsPayroll() function, not just the bracket math in
isolation. Treated as a high-confidence, primary-sourced,
worked-example-verified state, comparable to IN/NC/IA/DE.

Full self-test suite (all v1-v17 cases) passes. This closes out
state-by-state US payroll coverage: 50 states plus DC, each sourced
and self-tested, with every remaining confidence gap named explicitly
in this file's own limitations array rather than glossed over.
