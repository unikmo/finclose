payroll(ke,za): add Kenya and South Africa payroll engines (v1 draft, DRAFT_NEEDS_LEGAL_REVIEW)

Two more country payroll rule packs: lib/payroll-engine-ke.ts and
lib/payroll-engine-za.ts, wired into the shared dispatcher alongside
Georgia/Germany/US/UK/Canada.

Both are built from user-supplied "Payroll Implementation Reference"
documents (Kenya 2026 and South Africa 2026/27) citing KRA/NSSF/NITA
(Kenya) and SARS (South Africa) as primary authorities, with real
bands/rates/caps attributed to named government publications. Neither
was independently re-fetched from the primary government source
directly this pass.

Kenya v1: PAYE monthly bands (10%/25%/30%/32.5%/35%) with personal and
insurance relief; NSSF Tier I/II, correctly VERSIONED by pay_date
(Year 3 schedule before 2026-02-01, Year 4 schedule from 2026-02-01 --
a real mid-year statutory change the source explicitly warns about);
SHIF (2.75%, KES300 minimum); Affordable Housing Levy (1.5%/1.5%);
NITA (flat KES50/employee/month, employer-only). All 6 of the source
document's own worked golden-test figures are reproduced exactly by
this engine's self-tests: the NSSF Jan-vs-Feb transition (KES4,320 ->
KES6,480 per side on the same KES120,000 salary), the NSSF mid-band
case (KES3,000 employee NSSF on a KES50,000 salary from February), the
SHIF statutory minimum, the AHL split, and the NITA employer-only levy
across 50 employees.

South Africa v1: PAYE 2027 tax year brackets with age-based primary/
secondary/tertiary rebates and the medical scheme fees tax credit;
UIF (1%/1%, capped at R17,712/month remuneration -- the source's own
stated R177.12-per-side maximum is reproduced exactly); SDL (flat 1%
gated on a caller-supplied employer-liability flag, since the real
R500,000 12-month threshold test is not computed by this engine).
PAYE explicitly uses the same disclosed simplified annualize-and-
divide approximation already used for Germany/UK/Canada -- notably,
this is exactly what the source document itself explicitly warns
against doing ("Do not calculate monthly PAYE by merely applying 1/12
of annual brackets"), and this limitation is stated plainly in the
file's own limitations array rather than glossed over. ETI (the
hours-based employer PAYE incentive) and tax directives for severance/
lump-sum payments are NOT implemented -- a payment requiring a
directive must not be run through this engine.

Both packs are status DRAFT_NEEDS_LEGAL_REVIEW and gated behind
VERIFIED_BASIC_RULES in preparePayrollRun, same as every other pack.
Full aggregate self-test suite (Georgia + Germany + US + UK + Canada +
Kenya + South Africa) passes together.
